What to read

MusicXML — .musicxml, .mxl or .xml

Only a PDF of the piece? A PDF is a picture of music: nothing in it says which notes are which, so nothing could be marked right or wrong. Convert it first with Audiveris, which is free, runs on your own machine and writes MusicXML. Open the PDF in it, let it read the page, fix anything it got wrong, and export. MuseScore is also free and exports MusicXML from anything you can open or enter in it.

Difficulty

No keyboard found — you can still read, but nothing will be scored.
Two things this cannot do, so you know where you stand. It judges by which key you press, so it cannot tell F♯ from G♭ — they are the same key, and if the music says G♭ and you think “F♯”, it will say correct. And the right notes in the wrong octave count as wrong, though the review says so in those words rather than leaving you to work it out.
